PEER-TO-PEER, NOT TOWER-TO-PHONE

No cell tower, no satellite subscription, no ground infrastructure. Drones form the network the moment they power on.

STORE AND FORWARD

If no route exists, a node holds the payload in flash and re-transmits the instant a neighbour reappears. Detections are never lost — only delayed.

SELF-HEALING ROUTES

Losing a node does not partition the swarm. Neighbours detect the dead link within one beacon interval and re-run shortest-path around it.
